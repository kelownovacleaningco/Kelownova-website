Here's a bit about who we are and how we approach the work.

Our story

Kelownova Cleaning Co. started with a simple idea: cleaning is a personal service, and it should feel like one. We're a family-run business based right here in Kelowna, and we treat every home and workplace we clean the way we'd want our own treated — carefully, thoroughly, and without cutting corners.

As a small, local team, we're able to stay close to the details that make the biggest difference: showing up when we say we will, communicating clearly, and adjusting the plan as your needs change. We're building this business one relationship at a time, and we take that seriously.

What guides our work

A few things we hold ourselves to on every single visit.

Reliability

If we say we'll be there, we're there. Consistent scheduling matters as much as the clean itself.

Attention to detail

We follow the same thorough checklist every time, so quality doesn't depend on which day it is.

Honesty

Clear communication and transparent pricing — no surprises after the work is done.

Flexibility

Life and business schedules change. We work with you to adjust plans when they need to.

Care

Your space matters to you, so it matters to us — whether it's a family home or a small business.

Local roots

We're based in Kelowna and invested in this community, not a franchise operating at arm's length.
