Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer here? Send us a message.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Kelowna and the surrounding Okanagan, including West Kelowna, Lake Country and Peachland. If you're just outside these areas, reach out and we'll let you know if we can help.

Do you provide cleaning supplies?

We can discuss whether we bring supplies and equipment or use yours when we go over your quote, so everything is clear before your first visit.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Not necessarily. Many clients provide access instructions and go about their day. We'll work out an arrangement that feels comfortable for you.

How much does cleaning cost?

Pricing depends on the size and condition of your property, the type of cleaning and how often you'd like us to visit. Request a free quote and we'll give you a clear, upfront price.

Do you offer recurring cleaning?

Yes. We offer weekly, bi-weekly and monthly recurring plans in addition to one-time visits.

Do you clean Airbnb or short-term rental properties?

Yes, we offer turnover cleaning for short-term rentals, timed around your guest check-in and check-out schedule.

Do you offer deep cleaning?

Yes. Deep cleaning covers the detailed, less-frequent tasks that go beyond a standard clean — a great option for a seasonal reset or before a regular cleaning plan begins.

How do I request a quote?

Fill out the quote form on our Contact page or give us a call. We'll follow up to confirm details and provide a price.

Do you clean offices?

Yes, we offer office and small commercial cleaning on a schedule that works around your business hours.

How far in advance should I book?

It depends on the season and our current schedule. We recommend reaching out as early as you can, especially for move-in/move-out or one-time deep cleans with a fixed date.

Ready for a cleaner space?

Tell us a bit about your property and we'll get back to you with a free, no-obligation quote.
